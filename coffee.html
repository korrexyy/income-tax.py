Our Story

Roast & Leaf began as a small roastery focused on sustainable sourcing and transparent relationships with farmers. We roast weekly to highlight each origin's unique character — bright florals, chocolatey notes, or bold cocoa bitters.

Our Roast

We roast to clarity: balancing sweetness, acidity, and body. Our baristas treat each cup like a craft — precise, creative, and made to share.
